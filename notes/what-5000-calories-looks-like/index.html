What 5,000 calories a day actually looks like

It's the highest documented figure on the site. Written out as food rather than as a number, it stops sounding like an achievement.

12 Aug 20265 min readBuilt from Dwayne Johnson — off-season week

Five thousand calories is the number people screenshot. It sounds like abundance — eat whatever you want, twice. Written out meal by meal, it reads more like a shift rota.

This is the off-season week documented on the site, laid out as it was published:

4:45 amCod, oatmeal, egg whites (after cardio)780
8:00 amChicken, rice, broccoli820
11:00 amSteak, sweet potato, greens900
2:00 pmChicken, rice, asparagus820
5:00 pmFish, rice, vegetables780
8:30 pmCasein, egg whites, greens560

Six meals, and the first one is before five

The arithmetic is the least interesting part. What the layout tells you is that eating 5,000 calories of largely unprocessed food means eating roughly every three hours from before dawn until bedtime, and preparing all of it in advance.

Nobody hits this figure improvising. It requires a kitchen, a schedule with no unpredictable days in it, and somebody — often not the person eating — doing the cooking.

Why it's this high

A calorie figure is a function of three things: how much the person weighs, how much they train, and what they're trying to do. This is a large man training six days a week with the explicit intention of adding mass. Change any one of those inputs and the number stops applying.

Which is why lifting it out of context is the most reliable way to gain fat. At a lighter bodyweight and four training days, the same intake isn't a mass phase. It's just a large surplus with someone else's name attached to it.

The version that transfers

The structure is worth copying even when the number isn't. Protein at every meal, most of the carbohydrate placed around training, vegetables in nearly every plate, and one slow-digesting protein last thing. That pattern holds at 2,400 calories as well as at 5,000.

The calorie band is the useful unit, not the celebrity. The diet page is organised that way for exactly this reason, and the fridge tool will tell you which of these meals you can cook from what you already own.

None of this is medical or dietary advice. Programs here are documented, not prescribed.
